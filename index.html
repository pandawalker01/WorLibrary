CHAPTER IV · A LIFE, UNLIVED

One day, in the middle of a perfectly ordinary life, something extraordinary happened.

Adeline LaRue had always believed the world was made of doors. A door was not just a passage from one room to another; it was a promise. It was the chance that something on the other side might change the shape of her life.

But freedom, she would learn, is a word with a thousand meanings, and some of them are cages.

She did not know yet which doors would open for her, or which would close. She only knew that she had to keep walking.

翻译

但她后来会明白，自由这个词有一千种含义，其中有些，恰恰是牢笼。

通俗解释

这句话说的是：自由并不总是轻松美好的。有时我们以为自己在选择，却被选择的代价、规则或恐惧困住了。这里的“牢笼”不是实体，而是自由背后隐藏的限制。
